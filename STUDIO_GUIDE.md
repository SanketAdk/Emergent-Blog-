# Studio Guide (for the author)

The **Studio** is the private area where you write and publish. You don't need GitHub, code or JSON files any more.

## Logging in

1. Go to **`https://<your-site>/studio`**.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.

| If you see… | It means |
|-------------|----------|
| "Invalid username or password." | One of them is wrong. For security, the message doesn't say which. |
| "Too many failed attempts." | After 5 wrong tries the account pauses for **15 minutes** to stop password guessing. Just wait and try again. |

To change your password: click **@your-username** in the top bar → **Change password**.

## Writing an essay

1. Click **Essays → New essay**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the web address (the *URL slug*) is filled in for you.
   - **Excerpt**: one or two sentences shown on the home page and when the link is shared.
   - **Essay**: the text itself. Click **Preview** to see how it will look.
   - **Category** (optional): e.g. *Investing*, *Philosophy*. Readers can browse by category.
   - **Feature on the home page** (optional): shows the essay in a large box at the top. Only one essay can be featured at a time.
3. Choose a button:
   - **Publish** puts the essay live on the site within seconds.
   - **Save draft** saves it privately. Nobody else can see it.

### Formatting cheat-sheet

| Type this | To get |
|-----------|--------|
| A blank line between paragraphs | separate paragraphs |
| `## My heading` | a heading |
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` | *italic* |
| `[text](https://example.com)` | a link |
| `> a quote` | a block quote |
| `- item` on separate lines | a bullet list |

## Editing, unpublishing and deleting

- Open **Essays** and click the essay's title.
- **Update** saves your changes to a live essay.
- **Unpublish** takes it off the site but keeps it as a draft.
- **Delete essay** (at the bottom) removes it permanently. You'll be asked to confirm.

> ⚠️ Changing the **URL slug** of a published essay breaks links people have already shared.

## Documents (PDFs)

1. Click **Documents → Upload document**.
2. Choose **Upload a PDF** (maximum 10 MB) or **Link to an external document**, such as a Google Doc.
3. Add a title and description, then click **Publish** or **Save draft**.

Published documents appear on the **Documents** page of the site.

## Good to know

- If you try to close a page with unsaved changes, the browser will warn you first.
- The Studio is hidden from search engines.
- Always click **Log out** when you use a shared computer.
